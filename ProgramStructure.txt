Admission-time predictors: age, gender, primary diagnosis, comorbidity count,
planned surgery flag, insurance type, and social support.

Excluded: patient ID (identifier), ICU status and acquired infection (may be
known only after admission), and discharge delay (known near discharge). These
could be considered for a separate in-stay update model after timing is defined.

The input data has undocumented provenance. Random-split metrics are only an
educational estimate; they are not clinical or operational validation.
